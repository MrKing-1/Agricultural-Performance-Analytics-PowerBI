# 🌱Agricultural Performance & Yield Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a Power BI Agricultural Performance & Yield Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze farm productivity, crop performance, irrigation methods, water consumption, soil types, and seasonal trends.

The dashboard transforms agricultural data into actionable insights using Power Query for data cleaning and transformation, and Power BI for data modeling, visualization, KPI reporting, and analytical storytelling.

---

# Objectives

- Analyze overall farm productivity and yield performance.
- Identify high-performing crops and farming methods.
- Evaluate irrigation efficiency and water consumption patterns.
- Understand the relationship between farm area and yield.
- Compare seasonal production performance.
- Generate recommendations to improve agricultural efficiency.

# Key Insights

1. Drip irrigation produced the highest yield (402 tons), outperforming other irrigation methods and indicating improved water efficiency.

2. Tomato generated the highest yield while also recording the highest water consumption, showing strong production with higher resource requirements.

3. Zaid season recorded the highest average yield compared with other seasons, suggesting seasonal conditions influence productivity.

4. Top-performing farms achieved yields above 45 tons, showing significant productivity differences between farms.

5. Farm area showed a weak relationship with yield, suggesting operational factors such as irrigation, soil type, and input management influence productivity.

---

# Recommendations

1. Expand efficient irrigation methods such as drip systems to improve yield output and water efficiency.

2. Optimize water management for high-resource crops while maintaining productivity.

3. Analyze successful seasonal farming practices and apply them to lower-performing periods.

4. Identify high-performing farms and replicate successful operational strategies.

5. Improve productivity per acre through better crop selection, soil suitability, fertilizer usage, and irrigation decisions.
